
## Agencies and Brokerages

When you select a place to hang your license as a newly licensed salesperson, the name of the firm you work at may have “agency” in its name, e.g., “XYZ Agency.” However, no matter whether it refers to itself as “XYZ Agency” or “XYZ Brokers” or “XYZ Realty,” it is still a brokerage as well as an agency—they are synonymous. Let's learn more.
