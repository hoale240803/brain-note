## Welcome to What's My Status?

Regardless of your agency or non-agency relationship with a consumer, one other relationship you'll have to keep straight is the working relationship you have with your employing broker.

Ever hear of the classic game show, _What’s My Line?_  A contestant would visit the show and try to stump the panel—which could ask only questions that might be answered yes or no—about the contestant’s occupation.

We have our own version of that show in this section called _What’s My Status?_ You’ll be a panel of one, but instead of being able to ask questions, you’ll be given a description of each challenger and be asked to determine the status of the contestant based on the information you’ve been given. After you complete the game question, you'll complete the lesson as usual.

## What's My Status? First Challenger

Are you ready for your first challenger? Read this description and determine if our first challenger, Mr. Rob Brady, is an independent contractor or an employee.

Mr. Brady works for a real estate brokerage firm called Whosit Properties. The firm provides training and supervision. His work hours and the work he does aren't set by his firm. As long as Mr. Brady gets the job done, it doesn't matter how much time it takes or when he does the work. Mr. Brady isn't required to work in the office, and he must pay for his own work supplies.

Is Mr. Brady an independent contractor or an employee?

==🟢Independent contractor==
