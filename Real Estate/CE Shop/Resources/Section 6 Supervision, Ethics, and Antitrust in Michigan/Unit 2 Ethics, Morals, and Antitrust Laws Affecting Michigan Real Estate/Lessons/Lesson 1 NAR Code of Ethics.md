## NAR Code of Ethics

Whether or not you decide to become a member of the National Association of REALTORS® (NAR) as a real estate licensee, you need look no further than the **NAR Code of Ethics (COE)** for guidelines on the ethical practice of real estate.

>[!warning] Every profession that depends on trust (e.g., medicine, accounting, real estate) has established a code of ethics that **provides a framework for conduct** within that profession.

![[Pasted image 20261125113345.png]]

For many real estate licensees, that’s the NAR Code of Ethics.

> [!success] Although NAR is a member-based organization, the COE is **widely accepted as a guideline for professionals who serve the public**. It’s been referred to in court cases to help inform court decisions involving real estate professionals.

> [!note] There’s a link to the **NAR Code of Ethics and Standards of Practice** in your optional resources.

## Key of Ethics

Our goal is to give you **the keys you need to be successful** in your career as a real estate professional. Acting ethically is one of the keys to keeping your license and ensuring a long career.

>[!warning] But you’ve got to **earn your key**. How? You’ll start by completing the exercises in this lesson.

We’re providing you with a copy of the NAR Code of Ethics in your resources. It’s a long document, but it’s organized into duties, articles, and standards of practice that outline the ethical obligations of a REALTOR®.

![[Pasted image 20261125113456.png]]

We encourage you to **keep a copy of the COE nearby** and to familiarize yourself with the duties it describes.

> [!success] Now let’s get started with some questions about ethical actions. Answer them correctly, and **you’ll earn the Key of Ethics!**
