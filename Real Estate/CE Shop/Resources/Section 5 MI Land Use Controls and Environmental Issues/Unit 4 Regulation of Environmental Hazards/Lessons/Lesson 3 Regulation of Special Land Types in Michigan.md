
## Wetland, Floodplain, and Waterfront Property in Michigan

Wetlands provide many benefits besides being a habitat and breeding ground for many species of animals, birds, and fish. Wetlands act like a sponge for rain water. They help to filter and purify surface water, and help to balance the levels of nearby rivers and streams, thereby preventing flooding. They also help to stabilize soil and reduce erosion.
