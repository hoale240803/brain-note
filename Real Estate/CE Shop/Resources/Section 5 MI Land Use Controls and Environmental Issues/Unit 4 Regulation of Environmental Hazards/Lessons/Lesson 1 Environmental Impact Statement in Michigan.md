## The Environmental Impact Statement

Whenever development occurs, it impacts the environment. That’s one reason that the National Environmental Policy Act was created in 1970. This federal law, which also established the President’s Council on Environmental Quality, helped lay the groundwork for a national environmental policy. Chief among its provisions is the requirement that environmental assessments and environmental impact statements be conducted to determine the potential environmental effect of proposed federal agency actions.
