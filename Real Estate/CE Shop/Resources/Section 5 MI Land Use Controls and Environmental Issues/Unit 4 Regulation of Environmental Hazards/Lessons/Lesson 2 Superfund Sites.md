
## What’s a Superfund site?

You may have heard the term “Superfund site” before and wondered what it means. If you're working with a property that's in the vicinity of a Superfund site, this is something that needs to be disclosed.

When the EPA identifies an area as a Superfund site, it means that it presents a potential threat to human health or the environment as a result of uncontrolled hazardous waste. In blunt terms, it’s a toxic waste dump. In this lesson, you’ll learn about the law that's designed to identify such sites, determine liability for cleaning them up, and provide expertise and funds for that clean-up when necessary.

## CERCLA and SARA Summary

In this lesson, we looked at the ways in which Superfund sites affect communities and real property. The purpose of CERCLA is to address the identification and clean-up of hazardous waste sites. SARA amended CERCLA in a number of ways, including the creation of the innocent landowner immunity status.
