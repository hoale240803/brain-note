## Michigan Natural Resources and Environmental Protection Act (NREPA)

Michigan teems with natural resources: inland lakes and rivers, sand dunes, forests, fish, and wildlife. Probably Michigan’s greatest resources are by-products of the Great Lakes. However, according to Michigan’s Legislative Services Bureau, Science and Technology Division, the lakes are impacted by toxins, invasive species, and irresponsible development. The Michigan Natural Resources and Environmental Protection Act is designed to help protect the Great Lakes and Michigan’s other natural resources, in concert with the federal environmental protections we’ve already discussed.
