## Kelo vs. City of New London

[[Eminent Domain]]

Kelo vs. City of New London was a U.S. Supreme Court case that centered on a city’s use of the power of eminent domain. The resource briefly describes the focus of this case. What was significant about the court’s ruling?

The court ruled that the government can't take private property unless it's for a “public use.”

==🟢The court ruled that cities can exercise the power of eminent domain to promote private economic development, stating that the public will get an economic benefit.==

The court ruled that the government power of eminent domain is unconstitutional.

This case gave municipalities license to take private homes to increase development and provide a larger tax base for the city.
