## Types of Government Powers

The government imposes its power through taxation, eminent domain, escheat, and police power. Here’s a chart that provides a little more information about each power and whether the owners are compensated for their property.

We'd also like you to meet our friend PETE. No, PETE isn’t so full of himself that he demands to have his name spelled out in capital letters. PETE is an acronym for the government powers over land use:

P = Police power

E = Eminent domain

T = Taxes

E = Escheat

It's important to get to know PETE—because he's certainly going to get to know you.
