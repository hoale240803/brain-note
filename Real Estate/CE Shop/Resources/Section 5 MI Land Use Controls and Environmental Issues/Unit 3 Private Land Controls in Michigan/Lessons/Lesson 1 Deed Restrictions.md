
## What Is a Deed Restriction?

Welcome to this short lesson on deed restrictions. A deed restriction is a private agreement that impacts the use of the land. In this lesson, we focus on one type of deed restriction: the covenant.

You’ll receive a resource that will give you a concise description of how deed restrictions are used by land owners and developers.

## Deed Restrictions Recap

That wasn't too painful, now was it? Although you may have found it a bit restrictive ...

So the key takeaways for this lesson are that a deed restriction, like a lien, is another way to encumber a property. A covenant is a type of deed restriction. Covenants and deed restrictions can be part of the deed.

Covenants and deed restrictions can cover a wide range of items, from the types and heights of fencing, to the placement of satellite dishes and RVs, to having farm animals crowing, baaing, and grazing in your yard.

As a licensee, you'll want to make sure your clients are aware of any covenants and deed restrictions that have been imposed on a property they might be interested in buying. We're sure you would hate to have your clients move into their dream home only to find that they have to sell their RV, replace their satellite TV with cable, and find someone to take their pet sheep, Baabaa.
