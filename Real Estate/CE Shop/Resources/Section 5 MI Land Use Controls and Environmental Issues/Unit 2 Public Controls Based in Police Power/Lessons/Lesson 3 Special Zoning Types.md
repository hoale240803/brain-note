## What Zone Is This?

[[Special Zoning Types]]

Build the story using the following terms:


==🔵Cluster== zoning allows the developer to place residential properties closer together, allowing for large open spaces to be used for parks or other community amenities. ==🔵Cumulative zoning== allows any use that would be allowed in a higher-use, less-intensive zone to also be permitted in a lower-use, more-intensive zone.

==🔵Mixed-use zoning== combines complementary uses, such as residential and commercial. ==🔵Exclusive-use zoning== allows only one type of land use within a particular zone or district.

==🔵Spot zoning== is when a specific property is rezoned for another use within a zoning area.
