## Building Codes and City Certifications

Even if a development is approved, the developer isn’t off the hook just yet. Developers must comply with construction safety and sanitary standards, which can be found in the building code. Unless the developer builds at least to the building code standards, the building will not be issued a certificate of occupancy.
