
## Interstate Land Sales

What if you’re working with a developer selling property in another state? In addition to state subdivision laws, federal law may also apply. The Interstate Land Sales Full Disclosure Act regulates certain interstate sales or leases of undeveloped lots and requires developers to provide purchasers with specific information about the property before they buy. The law was formerly administered by the Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD), but responsibility was transferred to the **Consumer Financial Protection Bureau** in 2011.
