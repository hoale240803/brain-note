## That Rectangle Is a Square

Perhaps due to its early roots, honest mistakes, or fraudulent surveys, the PLSS system is not without its problems. New surveys must rely on established corners and surveys, flaws and all. This means that some sections are not a square mile and contain far fewer or more than the standard 640 acres!

In other areas, such as Ohio and Indiana, the numbering system used and starting point varies from the PLSS system in use in the rest of the U.S., because the PLSS system was not fully defined when these areas were surveyed.
