## severance

Altering the characteristic of an item from real property to personal property by removing it from the land

Ex. When Mrs. Goldman dug up her rose bushes, this was an act of severance. They were no longer part of the land, and were now personal property.

## annexation

An attachment of personal property to real property, thereby making it a fixture (also known as an attachment)

Ex. When Mrs. Goldman planted the rosebushes in her yard, the resultant annexation meant that the rosebushes were now made part of the property.
