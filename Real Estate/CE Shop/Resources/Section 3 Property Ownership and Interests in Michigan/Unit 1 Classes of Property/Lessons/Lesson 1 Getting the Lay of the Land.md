
## Getting the Lay of the Land

Let's look at some vocabulary terms that are foundational to your study of the real estate industry. Do you know the difference between the terms land, real estate, and real property? Even though those terms are often used interchangeably, there are some key differences. We're also going to explore the concept of personal property and how it differs from real property.

You'll also learn more about the bundle of rights associated with real property, which simply means the set of legal rights afforded to a title holder. This can include the rights of control, exclusion, enjoyment, and disposition. Rights can go all the way down to the center of the earth, and air rights can go all the way up to the stars.

In short, there's a "lot" to this lesson. Yeah, we had to say it.
