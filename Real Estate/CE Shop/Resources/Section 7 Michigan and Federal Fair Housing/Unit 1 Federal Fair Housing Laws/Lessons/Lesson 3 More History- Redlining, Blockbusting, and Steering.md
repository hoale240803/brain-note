## What Should Nancy Do?

Nancy has been a real estate salesperson for a couple of years, and she's still building her business. She has been noticing a demographic shift in the neighborhood where she lives. Many of the long-time residents are elderly white people and she's overheard them complaining several times about all the African Americans moving into the neighborhood.

The demographic shift plus the chatter around the neighborhood makes Nancy think there may be an opportunity to drum up business. What should she do?

She should take a proactive approach and knock on their doors. After a frank conversation about the changing nature of the neighborhood, she should let each owner know that now is a good time to sell. She should hint that prices may drop if they wait.

Advertise broadly, but make sure that all the images of families look Latino.

==🟢Make sure that all the listings mention each property’s proximity to parks, walking trails, and the community center.==
