## The End of a Career

When Cara’s boss asked her to “screen” out unsavory tenants, she gave in. She didn’t want to lose her position as property manager and didn’t know what else to do. Now she’s about to lose a whole lot more than just her job as a property manager. She called her mom to ask for help.

“Mom, I’m in trouble. You know that landlord I’ve been working for? He doesn’t like gays, or Jews, or anyone on assistance program. He said he’d fire me if I didn’t screen those applications out. So I did what he said."

"Cara! What were you thinking?!" her mom screamed.

"I know, Mom. I’m not proud of what I did."

"Well, you were just following the landlord's orders, right? They can't get you for that, can they?"

"Yes, I was just following orders, but I am the real estate professional. I’m supposed to know better. "

Next came the really hard part. Cara steeled herself and continued, "Mom, can I borrow some money? I’m going to need a lawyer. And there may be fines. The thing is, I don’t know when I can pay you back. If I lose my license, it might be awhile."

"Of course, honey. We'll do what we can. You don't think ... you're not going to go to jail for this, are you?"

"No. I don’t think I’ll go to jail. I’m meeting with my lawyer later today. I'll call you when the meeting's over."
