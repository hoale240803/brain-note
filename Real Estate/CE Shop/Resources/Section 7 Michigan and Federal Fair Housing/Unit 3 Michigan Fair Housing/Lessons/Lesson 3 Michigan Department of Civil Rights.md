## Michigan’s Civil Rights Regulators

Did you know Michigan was the first state in the country to adopt the rights of the individual as part of its state constitution? The Civil Rights Commission was established in 1964 and the following year, the Michigan Department of Civil Rights (MDCR) was created to serve as the enforcement arm of the Civil Rights Commission. Take a look at the brochure about the MDCR and the Civil Rights Commission in your resources.
