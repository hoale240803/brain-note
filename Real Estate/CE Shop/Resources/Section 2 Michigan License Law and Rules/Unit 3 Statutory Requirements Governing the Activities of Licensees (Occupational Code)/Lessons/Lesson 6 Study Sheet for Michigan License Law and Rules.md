## Michigan License Law and Rules Flashcards
### Broker 

An individual or entity licensed to operate a real estate business and supervise the activities of salespersons

### Salesperson

An individual licensed to act on behalf of a broker in real estate transactions

### License Law

The body of state laws that regulates the practice of real estate to protect the public

### Department of Licensing and Regulatory Affairs (LARA)

The Michigan state agency responsible for issuing and regulating professional licenses, including real estate

### Licensee

Any individual who holds a valid Michigan real estate broker or salesperson license

### Principal associate broker

An individual who holds a broker’s license and is designated by a firm as responsible for supervising other licensees

### Branch office

A secondary location apart from the main office where the broker conducts real estate business

### Pocket card

A portable proof of licensure issued by the department to brokers and salespersons

### Place of business

The physical office location where a broker conducts real estate activities and maintains required records

### License renewal

The process by which a licensee maintains an active status by meeting education and filing requirements before expiration

### Inactive license

A license status in which an individual is not authorized to engage in real estate activities

### Revocation

The permanent cancellation of a real estate license as a disciplinary action

### Suspension

A temporary removal of a licensee’s right to practice real estate

### Cease and desist order

A directive issued by the department requiring an individual to stop unlicensed or improper real estate activity

### Misrepresentation

Making a false statement or withholding important information in a real estate transaction

### Commingling

Mixing a client’s or customer’s funds with the broker’s personal or business funds

### Trust account

A separate bank account used by brokers to hold client or customer funds until properly disbursed

### Material fact

Information that would influence a buyer’s or seller’s decision in a real estate transaction

### Fiduciary duty

The obligation of a licensee to act in the best interests of their client with loyalty and honesty

### Continuing education

Ongoing coursework required to maintain an active real estate license

### Complaint

A formal written allegation filed with the department against a licensee for possible violations

### Hearing

A formal proceeding in which a licensee may respond to alleged violations of the license law
