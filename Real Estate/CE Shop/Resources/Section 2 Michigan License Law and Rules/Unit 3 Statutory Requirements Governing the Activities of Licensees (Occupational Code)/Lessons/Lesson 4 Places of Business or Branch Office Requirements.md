
## Broker’s Place of Business

Let’s talk shop. Rather, let’s talk shop _location_. Brokers are required under Michigan law (section §339.22323) to maintain a place of business in the state of Michigan. Brokers are allowed to have branch offices, but if they do, each branch office has to carry its own license. What does the law say specifically about what constitutes a branch office? Let’s explore and find out.
