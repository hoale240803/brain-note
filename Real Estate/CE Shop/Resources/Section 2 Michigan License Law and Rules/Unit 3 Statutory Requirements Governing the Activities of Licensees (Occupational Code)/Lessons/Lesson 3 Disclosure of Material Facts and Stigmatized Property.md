## Stigmatized Property

It may surprise you to learn that stigmatization is not a material fact. A house could’ve been previously owned or occupied by a serial killer and neither the seller, their salesperson, nor their broker are under any obligation to disclose that information to potential buyers. Let’s find out what constitutes a stigmatized property, and what the law says about disclosure.

## Stigmatized Property and the Law

Michigan state law says information about stigmatized property is not material, so it’s not required that it be disclosed. There is an exception to the rule: when the information can affect the property’s marketability or value. Which of the following stigma-inducing situations could affect the property’s marketability or value?

There are four correct answers. 

==🟢63 cats were removed when the former occupant moved out==

==🟢The basement floor was dug up, looking for buried bodies==

==🟢The windows were all replaced after a meth lab in the kitchen exploded==

==🟢The prior occupant tore apart the walls and tried to repair himself==
