## Conditions to Receiving Commission

In this lesson, we'll focus on commissions earned through real estate activities and what types of payments are and are not allowed.

## Commissions

[[Commissions and Specific Services for Which Fees May Be .pdf]]

Identify whether these commission-related statements are true or false.

If a salesperson earned a commission while licensed and associated with a broker, the broker can still pay that commission to the salesperson regardless of whether the salesperson is still licensed at the time of payment, or is employed by another broker.
==🟢True==

An owner or owner’s agent can offer existing tenants of a property compensation for referring a prospective tenant. The compensation can’t exceed the value of a half months’ rent.
==🟢False==

Licensees receiving commission from a transaction can’t also receive a referral fee or other compensation related to a principal securing a loan for the transaction, unless both buyer and seller provide prior written consent and the payment doesn’t violate the Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act (RESPA) or other laws.
==🟢True==
