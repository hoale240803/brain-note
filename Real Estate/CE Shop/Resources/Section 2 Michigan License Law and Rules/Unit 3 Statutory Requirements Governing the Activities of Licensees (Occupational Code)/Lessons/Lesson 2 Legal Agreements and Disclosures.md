## Disclosure of Agency Relationship (Service Provision Agreement)

All clients need to know they can trust you to be loyal, honest, thorough, and compliant with the law. Michigan law requires a disclosure of agency relationships available in Michigan before a consumer provides any confidential information to the licensee. This disclosure consists of the following:

- Minimum duty and service requirements of the licensee
- Definition of the potential agency relationships of a licensee:
    1. Seller’s agent
    2. Buyer’s agent
    3. Dual agent
- Disclosure to potential buyers and sellers of the licensee’s agency relationships, and the responsibilities of each relationship

While the disclosure identifies the relationships available, it's the service provision agreement that establishes the agency relationship between the client and licensee. You may hear the service provision agreement also referred to as an agency agreement. Check out your resource to learn more.
