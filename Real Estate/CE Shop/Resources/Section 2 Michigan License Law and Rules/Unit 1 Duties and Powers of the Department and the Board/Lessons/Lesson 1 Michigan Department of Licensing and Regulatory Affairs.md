1.Regulation of Occupational Professions

Real estate is one of 15 occupational professions regulated in Michigan by the licensing division, along with the state licensing board for each profession. It can be difficult to understand the structure of the state licensing and regulatory affairs agency, so we’re going to take a few minutes and break it down for you in this lesson

2.LARA

The Department of Licensing and Regulatory Affairs (LARA) oversees more than 1.2 million individual and institutional licenses. The department serves to support business growth and the creation of new jobs by making sure Michigan has an efficient and fair regulatory structure. We’ve provided a breakdown of LARA’s organizational structure in your resources. Let’s get started.

The chief deputy director is responsible for the oversight of the Bureau of Professional Licensing, which is in turn responsible for the oversight of the Board of Real Estate Brokers and Salespersons.
