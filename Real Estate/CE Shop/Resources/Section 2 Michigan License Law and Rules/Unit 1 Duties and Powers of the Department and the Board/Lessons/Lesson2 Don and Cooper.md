Don Flores was meeting his long-time friend, Cooper Fayed, for lunch. Cooper’s a broker and a former member of the Board of Real Estate Brokers and Salespersons.

“Coop!” Don quickly stood up from his chair as he saw his friend approaching.

“Hey, man!” Cooper shook Don’s hand.

As the two men settled in and ordered their lunch, Don explained his interest in being a member of the board.

“I enjoyed my time on the board,” Cooper said. “Tell me why you’re interested.”

“I’ve been a broker for almost as long as you have, and I’d like to be a part of the board so I can help pave the way for the next generation.”

“Sounds like a good reason. If I could appoint you myself, I would, but that has to come from the governor.”

“Can you remind me about the board’s make-up?”

“Sure. There are nine official members. Six are licensed real estate brokers, and the remaining three are members of the general public. All nine are appointed by the governor.”
