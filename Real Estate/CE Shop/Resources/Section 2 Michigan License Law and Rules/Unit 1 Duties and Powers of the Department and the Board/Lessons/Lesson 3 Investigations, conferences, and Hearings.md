## The Contested Case Hearing

If an informal conference weren’t held, or resolution didn’t come from an informal conference, the case proceeds to a contested case hearing. This is the final step in the complaint process before the licensee can be assessed penalties.

Which of the following statements are true about the contested case hearing? Choose either true or false for each statement.

A contested case hearing must be held for every complaint filed. <=> FALSE

If resolution didn’t come from an informal conference, a contested case hearing must be held. <=> TRUE

The Department of Licensing and Regulatory Affairs or the attorney general may petition the circuit court to subpoena individuals for the contested case hearing. <=> TRUE

The contested case hearing results in a final penalty decision. <=> FALSE
