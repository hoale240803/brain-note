
## License Renewal in Michigan

[[Michigan License Renewal Requirements]]


Now that you know what it takes to get a license in Michigan, let’s explore what it takes to maintain each of the different license types.

## Recent Legal Changes

On February 13, 2024, changes to Michigan's real estate license law came into effect, refining some of the state's CE requirements for licensees.

Beginning with the first license cycle after this date, the number of hours of required CE is the number of years in the license cycle, multiplied by six (in other words, a minimum of 18 hours). CE hours must include:

- At least two hours per year on laws, rules, and court cases regarding real estate
- At least one hour per year on compliance with local, state, or federal fair housing laws

For the rest of their required CE hours, a licensee may choose any courses that are in their area of expertise, and they may complete the hours anytime during the license cycle.

When attending a CE course, a licensee must be able to prove their identity by providing both of the following:

- Either their pocket card or their real estate license number
- A Michigan driver's license, a Michigan state ID card, or other government-issued ID

If a licensee wants to request a waiver of CE requirements, the Department of Licensing and Regulatory Affairs must receive the request at least 30 days before the last board meeting that takes place before the licensee's license expires.

## Recent Legal Changes (continued)

A licensee who completes a course to obtain a professional designation may count those hours toward their CE requirements for that license cycle. A licensee may not claim credit for repeating a course they already completed successfully.

A licensee must retain records of successful CE course completion for 4 years, and must provide the following information to the department upon request:

- Name and contact information for the CE program sponsor
- The participant's name
- The course title and field of study
- The date the course was offered or completed
- The location of the course (if applicable)
- Verification of course completion by a representative of the CE program
- The number of hours in the course

The department may prorate CE requirements who became licensed after the cycle began. However, this proration only applies to year of the license cycle in which the license was issued. Completed CE credits do not apply toward broker pre-licensing requirements, and completed broker pre-licensing education credits do not apply toward CE requirements.

If the department finds an applicant for license renewal is short of CE hours, any penalty imposed must include completing sufficient CE to be in compliance.

## Broker/Salesperson CE Requirements

What is the CE requirement for brokers, associate brokers, and salespersons?

14 hours of CE annually

28 hours of CE every two years after the first renewal

==🟢18 hours of CE every three years, including two hours of legal updates per year==
