
## Types of Licenses in Michigan

Michigan has three different types of real estate licenses: associate broker, broker, and salesperson. Let’s review the eligibility, education, and experience requirements for each. We'll finish out this lesson focusing on independent contractor requirements in Michigan.

## Real Estate Roles for Michigan Licensees

You’ll find Michigan real estate licensees working as salespersons, associate brokers, and brokers. Match the title to the description to show what you know. There’s a resource for your review.

A person who holds an entry-level license and performs real estate services under the supervision of a licensed broker <=> ==🟢Salesperson==

A licensed broker who chooses to work under the supervision of another licensed broker <=> ==🟢Associate broker=

A person who performs real estate services for others for compensation <=> ==🟢Broker==


## The Broker License

Lesson Resources

[[Licensed Brokers in Michigan.pdf]]

One of the things that sets the broker license apart from the salesperson license is that a broker license sets licensees up to manage their own businesses. Licensed brokers in Michigan can choose to operate as an associate broker under a licensed broker, as a sole proprietor, or as a sponsoring broker who runs a real estate office and oversees the work of other salespersons and associate brokers.
