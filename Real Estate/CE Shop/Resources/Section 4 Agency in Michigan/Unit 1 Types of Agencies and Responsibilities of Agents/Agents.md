Principal: Someone who's a party to a transaction OR the person for whom the agent acts

Customer: Someone who's working with a real estate licensee, but isn't represented by him

Consumer: Someone who uses or purchases a product or service

Client: Someone who has agency representation with the real estate licensee


Agency Relationship Definitions
Agent: Someone who acts on behalf of someone else
Fiduciary: Someone who's in a position of trust and loyalty
Non-agent: Someone who represents neither party in the transaction
