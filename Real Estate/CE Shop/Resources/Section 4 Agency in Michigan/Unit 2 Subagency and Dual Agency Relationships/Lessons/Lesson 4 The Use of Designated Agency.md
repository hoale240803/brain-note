## Introduction to the Use of Designated Agency

Many firms decide to become designated agency offices, and require consent from all of their clients to accept designated agency terms before the start of any relationship. This is done regardless of whether or not the client ultimately buys a listing from the same brokerage. It’s basically preempting any dual agency situation that may arise. Even so, before the broker enters into a dual agency situation, the parties should be reminded what this means.  

If your brokerage practices designated agency, with your broker acting as dual agent and each party being represented by individual agents, is that enough to ensure confidentiality between the parties? Perhaps, perhaps not. Let’s talk more.
