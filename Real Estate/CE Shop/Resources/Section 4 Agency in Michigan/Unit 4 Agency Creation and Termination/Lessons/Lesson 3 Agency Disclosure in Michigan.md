## When Must the Agency Disclosure Be Made?

Agency Disclosure in Michigan

When is the licensee required to make the agency disclosure?

During the first meeting you have with your client after you have entered into a contractual agreement with them

==🟢Before a prospective buyer or seller reveals confidential information==

Just before you review the sales contract with your client

Any time prior to closing, but before you receive your commission for your part in the transaction

## Why Make the Disclosure?

Why is the licensee required to make the agency disclosure?

It's an effective way to position your client's interest ahead of the other party's interest.

It's the law.

It's the polite thing to do.

It's a good idea for everyone involved in a transaction to know each other well.
