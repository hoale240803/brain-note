
## The Duty of Loyalty

"Hi. It’s me, Keira, your favorite real estate professional. The next fiduciary duty in the OLD CAR is L for loyalty. This is one of the most fundamental fiduciary duties I owe my clients—it underlies all of the other duties. "Let's spend some time discussing this duty."

Agents must act in their clients’ best interest at all times—above the interests of all others, including the agent’s own.

Example

Sherman represents seller June, and a potential buyer for the property offers to let Sherman list his house if Sherman can get June to lower her price. Sherman must refuse, since his loyalty belongs to the client from the moment both parties agree to an agency relationship.
