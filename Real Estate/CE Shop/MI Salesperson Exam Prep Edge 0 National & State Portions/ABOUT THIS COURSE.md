## The Initial Assessment

After completing these introductory lessons, the next step is to take your initial assessment. This assessment isn’t intended to replicate a licensing exam. Instead, its purpose is to gauge your current knowledge level for each of the topics that make up the licensing exam. Because it’s gauging your starting point, you can only take the initial assessment once.

Tip: Dedicate at least an hour to complete the initial assessment in one sitting.

## Initial Assessment Results

Your initial assessment results will give you an overall score for the assessment, but more importantly than that, you’ll see how you scored in each topic area. These are the same topics that appear on your licensing exam. Topics are color-coded based on your performance, so you can easily see which topics may require more studying than others.

- **Red:** Score of 60% or less
- **Yellow:** Score between 60% and 80%
- **Green:** Score above 80%

**Tip**: Use these results to start working on your study plan and to determine which topics you may wish to tackle first.
